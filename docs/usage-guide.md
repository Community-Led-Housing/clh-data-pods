# Community-Led Housing Metadata Schema

The Community-Led Housing Metadata Schema is a shared framework for describing cooperative and community-driven housing initiatives. It supports:

- 🏘️ Local groups documenting their project or legal form  
- 🧭 Policymakers and funders comparing initiatives across regions  
- 🧑‍💻 Developers building tools, platforms, and dashboards  
- 🏛️ Researchers and federations structuring comparative data  

---

## Why this schema?

Community-led housing initiatives are diverse. Yet, across contexts, they often face similar challenges:

- How to explain their legal form to outsiders?  
- How to trace affordability and collective ownership structures?  
- How to align with national or EU-level housing frameworks?

This schema offers a **modular, open standard** to help solve these challenges — transparently and collaboratively.

---

## What does it include?

- ✅ **Core schema**: describe type, legal form, ownership, and structure
- ✅ **Controlled vocabularies**: consistent terms for legal entity types, contract roles, and member functions
- 🧩 **Extensions (coming soon)**: real estate, compliance, social context, and governance layers

---

## Who can use it?

- 📊 Cooperative networks
- 🏛️ Municipal or national housing authorities
- 🤝 Community organisers and federations
- 🧱 Technical toolmakers
- 🎓 Academic or policy researchers

No coding required — just structured clarity.
